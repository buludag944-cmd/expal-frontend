Your Personal Public Service (PPS) number is one of the first things you will need after moving to Ireland. Employers, Revenue, doctors and most public services ask for it. Here is how to get yours without wasted trips or rejected uploads.

## What is a PPS number?

A PPS number is your unique reference number for dealing with public services in Ireland. It is always **7 numbers followed by one or two letters**.

You will typically need it to:

- start a job and be taxed correctly
- access medical services
- apply for or exchange a driving licence
- apply for a social welfare payment
- register for a course at a school or college

You do **not** need a PPS number to look for work. You apply once you have a reason, such as a signed job offer.

## Who can apply

- People living in Ireland aged 18 or over apply online at [MyWelfare.ie](https://www.mywelfare.ie).
- Parents or guardians apply for children under 18 using form REG1M.
- People living outside Ireland who need a number to deal with an Irish state agency use form REG1.
- People who arrived from Ukraine under the EU Temporary Protection Directive should not apply online. They should contact a PPS Number Allocation Centre instead.

## Step 1: Gather your three documents

Every application needs proof of **identity**, proof of **address** and proof of **why you need the number**. Getting these right is the main reason applications go smoothly.

**Proof of identity**

- Non-EU/EEA citizens: your current passport.
- EU/EEA citizens: your current passport or national identity card.
- Expired documents, employment ID cards and personal letters are not accepted.

**Proof of address**

- It must show your name and address and be no more than 3 months old.
- Accepted examples: a household utility bill, an official letter, a financial statement, a lease or tenancy agreement, or a letter from a third party such as a hostel manager, school principal or property owner.
- Staying with friends or family? Upload an original household bill in their name plus a signed note from the bill holder confirming you live there.

**Proof of why you need it**

- For work: a signed job offer on company-headed paper, with the employer's contact details and registration number, stating when the job starts or started.
- For study: confirmation of your course registration.

## Step 2: Apply online at MyWelfare

Complete the PPS number application at [MyWelfare.ie](https://www.mywelfare.ie) in full and upload all your documents. Make sure every scan or photo is clear and easy to read, because blurry uploads are a common reason applications get sent back.

## Step 3: Attend your in-person appointment

After reviewing your application, the Department of Social Protection contacts you with an appointment. **An in-person appointment is required.** Bring the originals of the documents you uploaded.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Uploading a proof of address older than 3 months.
- A job offer that is unsigned, not on company paper, or missing the start date.
- Blurry or cropped photos of your passport.
- Applying before you have a valid reason. Get your job offer or course registration first.

## What to do next

Once you have your PPS number, give it to your employer and set up your tax with Revenue so you are not put on emergency tax. If you are from outside the EU/EEA, also make sure you register your immigration permission within 90 days of arriving.

## FAQ

**How long is a PPS number?**
It is always 7 numbers followed by one or two letters.

**Can I get a PPS number before I have a job?**
You need a valid reason to apply, such as a signed job offer, a course registration or a need to access public services. You don't need one to look for work.

**Do I need an appointment?**
Yes. You apply online first, and the department then contacts you with an in-person appointment.
